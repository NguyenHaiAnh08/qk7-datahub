HUONG DAN CAI QK7 FINGERPRINT AGENT CHO MAY CON
================================================
(Tai lieu nay duoc viet dua tren ma nguon: build_exe.py, build_setup.py,
installer_setup.py, agent.py. Chua duoc thu tren may that.)


1. TONG QUAN
------------
QK7AgentSetup.exe cai "QK7 Fingerprint Agent" len may con, de trang QK7 DataHub
doc duoc van tay tu dau doc ZKTeco ZK9500 cam vao may do.

- Khong can quyen admin.
- Khong can cai Python tren may con.
- Bo cai KHONG cai driver ZKTeco. Driver phai cai tay (xem muc 3).

May con moi can du 3 thu:
  a) Dau doc ZK9500 da cam USB va da cai driver ZKTeco.
  b) Duong mang toi may chu 192.168.1.229 qua cong 8000.
  c) File QK7AgentSetup.exe da build san tu may chu (muc 2).


2. PHAN A - CHUAN BI TREN MAY CHU VA BUILD BO CAI (lam mot lan)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Build mot lan la dung duoc cho moi may con. Bo cai da goi san: dia chi may chu,
BRIDGE_TOKEN, rootCA.pem, cert.pem va private-key.pem cua agent.

May build can co:
  1. BRIDGE_TOKEN trong backend/.env, dai tu 16 ky tu tro len, va GIONG token
     dang chay tren may chu.
  2. fingerprint/cert.pem va fingerprint/private-key.pem.
     Tao bang:  mkcert 127.0.0.1 localhost   (roi doi ten file thanh
     cert.pem / private-key.pem).
  3. rootCA.pem cua mkcert, mac dinh o:
     %LOCALAPPDATA%\mkcert\rootCA.pem
  4. Python co pyinstaller va cac thu vien trong fingerprint/requirements.txt.

Chay lenh trong thu muc fingerprint:

    python build_setup.py --server 192.168.1.229

Ket qua: fingerprint\dist\QK7AgentSetup.exe

Luu y:
  - Backend tren may chu phai la ban moi co agent-crypto.js va fingerprint-ws.js,
    neu khong agent moi se khong xac thuc duoc voi may chu.
  - BRIDGE_TOKEN nam ngay trong file exe: chi chia se file nay trong mang noi bo
    tin cay.
  - Doi IP may chu thi phai build lai (dia chi da goi san trong exe).
  - Chi can copy file QK7AgentSetup.exe. KHONG copy installer_setup.py: do chi
    la ma nguon, no can QK7FingerprintAgent.exe di kem.


3. PHAN B - CHUAN BI TREN MAY CON MOI
-------------------------------------
  [ ] He dieu hanh Windows.
  [ ] Dau doc ZKTeco ZK9500 da cam vao cong USB.
  [ ] Da cai driver ZKTeco (bo ZKFinger SDK / driver cua hang).
      Day la viec duy nhat phai cai tay.
  [ ] May con truy cap duoc may chu: mo https://192.168.1.229:8000 tren trinh
      duyet cua may con de thu.

Khong can cai Python, khong can quyen admin, khong can copy file nao ngoai
QK7AgentSetup.exe. Dung copy device_id.txt tu may khac: moi may phai co ma
thiet bi rieng.


4. PHAN C - CAC BUOC CAI DAT
----------------------------
  1. Copy QK7AgentSetup.exe sang may con (USB hoac thu muc chia se noi bo).
  2. Cam dau doc ZK9500 va dam bao driver da nhan thiet bi.
  3. Chay QK7AgentSetup.exe, bam Yes khi hoi cai vao %LOCALAPPDATA%\QK7Agent.
  4. Bo cai tu lam: chep agent va Uninstall.exe vao thu muc tren, tao shortcut
     o Start Menu va Desktop, dang ky tu chay cung Windows, them muc go cai dat
     vao Settings > Apps, roi khoi dong agent.
  5. Agent mo mot cua so console. GIU CUA SO NAY MO trong luc dung.
  6. Lan dau, neu Windows hoi cai chung chi, bam Yes, roi dong va mo lai
     trinh duyet.

Cai khong hoi:      QK7AgentSetup.exe /silent
Tu cac lan dang nhap Windows sau, agent tu chay lai.


5. PHAN D - KIEM TRA SAU KHI CAI
--------------------------------
  Tren may con   : mo https://127.0.0.1:9001/device-id
                   -> thay ma thiet bi nghia la agent dang chay.
  Tren may chu   : mo /api/fingerprint/agents-online
                   -> danh sach co may con vua cai.
  Tren may con   : chay fingerprint\kiem-tra-ket-noi.bat (neu co)
                   -> khong bao loi ket noi hay chung chi.

Cua so console cua agent hien dong "WS connected" la binh thuong.


6. PHAN E - LOI THUONG GAP
--------------------------
Dau hieu                                   | Nguyen nhan / Cach xu ly
-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------
"Server khong xac thuc duoc (sai           | Token trong exe khac may chu.
BRIDGE_TOKEN...)"                          | -> Build lai bo cai voi token dung.
Thieu driver / khong thay ZK9500           | Chua cai driver hoac chua cam USB.
                                           | -> Cai driver, cam lai, chay lai.
Trinh duyet canh bao chung chi             | Chua tin chung chi goc may chu.
                                           | -> Bam Yes khi Windows hoi, mo lai
                                           |    trinh duyet.
Agent khong noi duoc may chu               | Sai IP trong exe hoac cong 8000 bi
                                           | chan. -> Kiem tra mang; doi IP thi
                                           | build lai.
Cong 9001 bi chiem                         | Agent tu tat tien trinh dang giu cong
                                           | do. Chay lai neu can.
Hai may cung ma thiet bi                   | Da copy device_id.txt tu may khac.
                                           | -> Xoa device_id.txt, agent tao ma
                                           |    moi.


7. FILE QK7AgentSetup.exe BI PHAN MEM DIET VIRUS CANH BAO
---------------------------------------------------------
Thuong la canh bao nham, nhung can kiem tra. Ly do thuong gap:
  - File khong co chu ky so (code signing), nen SmartScreen khong biet nha
    phat hanh.
  - Dong goi bang PyInstaller dang mot file: ma doc cung hay dung cach nay.
  - Hanh vi giong phan mem doc hai: tu dang ky chay cung Windows (khoa Run),
    taskkill, goi PowerShell tao shortcut, mang theo chung chi va token.
  - Tai qua trinh duyet bi gan nhan "tai tu Internet".

Cach xu ly, tu an toan den it an toan:
  1. Dua file len VirusTotal. 1-3 cong cu bao ten chung chung (Generic, Heur)
     thuong la canh bao nham. Nhieu cong cu noi tieng cung bao thi dung lai va
     build lai tu ma goc tren may sach.
  2. Chuyen file bang thu muc chia se noi bo hoac USB, khong tai qua trinh duyet.
  3. Them ngoai le CO PHAM VI HEP cho dung file hoac thu muc
     %LOCALAPPDATA%\QK7Agent. Dung tat diet virus toan may.
  4. Lau dai: ky so file bang chung chi code signing (voi mang noi bo co the dung
     chung chi do CA noi bo cap va phan phoi chung chi do cho cac may).

Gui kem ma bam de nguoi nhan doi chieu:
    certutil -hashfile QK7AgentSetup.exe SHA256


8. GO CAI DAT VA GHI CHU BAO MAT
--------------------------------
Go cai dat: vao Settings > Apps, go "QK7 Fingerprint Agent", hoac chay:
    QK7AgentSetup.exe /uninstall
Thao tac nay tat agent, xoa shortcut, bo muc tu chay va xoa thu muc
%LOCALAPPDATA%\QK7Agent.

Ghi chu bao mat:
  - File exe chua BRIDGE_TOKEN. Chi chia se trong mang noi bo tin cay, khong gui
    qua kenh cong khai.
  - Chi copy rootCA.pem (phan cong khai), KHONG BAO GIO copy rootCA-key.pem.
  - Neu nghi ngo token bi lo: doi BRIDGE_TOKEN tren may chu, build lai va cai lai
    cho tat ca may con.
